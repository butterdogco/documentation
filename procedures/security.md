Our security procedures, such as how you can report security concerns, and how we handle security related concerns. Note that any sort of concern you have may be reported to us, big or small, even if you aren't sure. It's best for us to know!

## Reporting a Security Concern

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in any of our services, please report it to keegan(at)butterdogco.com (replace the (at) with @). We ask that you include as much information as possible when reporting the vulnerability to us. All security concerns should idealy be __reported privately__, preferably through the email mentioned previously, or some other private method such as a direct-message on Discord to a ButterDogCo Admin.

When we recieve a security report, or find out through other means *(please report them to us first!)*, we act ASAP. Typically, we follow the following:

1. Identify the risk or damage-- should the related services be shutdown for the time being?
2. Restrict access or shutdown things (if needed).
3. Identify and fix the problem(s). If we don't have control, such as it being a third party hosting provider's fault, find solutions and perform actions necessary to retain security.

### Why Should You Report a Security Concern?

We do our best to maintain a strong level of security for our users, as we don't want people's private information getting leaked. Unfortunately, you don't get anything big for reporting something to us, maybe just a thank you and credits posted somewhere after we believe it is safe to publicly announce the report. This can be discussed privately after the problem has been resolved.
