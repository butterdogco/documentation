As per our [Privacy Policy][Privacy Policy], you have the right to request access to the personal information we collect from you, change that information, and delete it. Contact information for such requests can be found in the [Review, Update, or Delete Your Data section of the Privacy Policy][Privacy Policy: Deletion].

## Submitting Such a Request

You may submit a request for any of the following:

1. Information about categories of personal information (if any) we disclosed to third parties;
2. deleting data related to you in services related to ButterDogCo (e.g., ButterDog Chat);
3. any other reason legally given to you via applicable laws of your country. 

In these requests, we ask that you please include as much of the following information as applicable to your usage of our services:

- Your name or username(s) you've used across our services;
- identifiers(s) (email(s), phone number(s)) you've used across our services;
- which service(s) you've registered an account under (e.g., ButterDog Chat).

Please keep in mind that your submissions to our forms, such as for ButterTube or Da Hub Requests, are also included in data deletion requests, and we ask that you mention your usage of these for a quick and easy data deletion on our end.

You can expect a response within 2 to 3 business days,

## Handling Such Requests

When handling such requests, we follow the steps below:

1. Identify the services you've used based on the information you provide to us;
2. lookup and perform the requested actions on the relevant databases;
3. double check our work and report back to you on our progress.

Our report back to you will include the following information:

- What data we found with the information provided;
- what was performed on the data found (if any);
- any additional information we need (if any).

## Future Plans

In the future, we hope to provide an easy-to-use web interface for submitting and viewing the status of such requests. However, at this time we do not have a predicted date for the release of such an interface-- it is only a concept.
